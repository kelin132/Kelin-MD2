Pet parity findings from `/home/ubuntu/Kelin-MD2/lib/petDatabase.js`, `plugins/pets/petshop.js`, and `lib/petData.js`:

- Collection is `pets`; each document uses `owner`, `petId`, `name`, `species`, `rarity`, `level`, `exp`, `expNeeded`, `hp`, `maxHp`, `attack`, `defense`, `speed`, `hunger`, `happiness`, `imageUrl`, `skill`, `isActive`, `lastFed`, and `lastPlayed`.
- `.hatch` caps a trainer at 5 pets, rolls rarity weights common 40, uncommon 28, rare 18, epic 9, legendary 4, mythic 1, and creates a 5-digit petId.
- `.petshop` prices/effects: kibble 200 (+40 hunger), meal 500 (hunger 100 and +10 happiness), toy 300 (+35 happiness), exppotion 800 (+150 EXP), revival 600 (+60 hunger and +40 happiness).
- EXP progression is `Math.floor(100 * Math.pow(level, 1.3))`; stat scale is `1 + (level - 1) * 0.08`.
- Card ownership is stored in `mn_users` with canonical owner `userId` and array `cards`; card entries include `cardId`, `name`, `tier`, `tierNum`, `index`, `spawnId`, `price`, `series`, `media`, `mediaType`, and `obtainedAt`.
